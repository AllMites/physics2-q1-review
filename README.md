# Physics 2 — Quarter 1 Review Deck

Interactive review slides covering the second half of Physics 2 Quarter 1:
waves and simple harmonic motion.

**Live deck:** https://allmites.github.io/physics2-q1-review/

## Contents

1. Wave Diffraction
2. Wave Interference
3. Superposition of Waves
4. Introduction to Simple Harmonic Motion
5. Energy and Simple Harmonic Motion
6. Sinusoidal Nature of Simple Harmonic Motion
7. The Simple Pendulum

Plus a 12-question self-check and a printable cheat sheet (two columns, about
two to three A4 pages).

## Using it

Arrow keys move between slides, up/down jump between topics, `F` toggles
fullscreen, and `Ctrl+P` prints the cheat sheet and nothing else. Everything is one
self-contained HTML file with no network dependency, so it works offline.

All nine simulations compute real physics rather than replaying an animation.
The pendulum integrates the exact equation of motion, so the small-angle
approximation visibly breaks down past about 20 degrees.

## Sources

Built from the PSHS-MC KHub Physics 2 learning guides (Manuel): Activities 1.7,
1.8 and 2.4, and Lessons 1.9, 2.1, 2.2, 2.3 and 2.5. Problems are drawn from
Giancoli, *Physics: Principles with Applications*; Young & Freedman,
*University Physics*; Cutnell & Johnson, *Physics*; and Gatch, *Test Item File*.
Credit for all source material remains with the original authors.

Two graph-reading exercises and one energy diagram use substitute values,
because the numbers in the originals exist only inside images. Those slides say
so. Section-title artwork is AI-generated decoration and carries no physics.
